The interface's sounds are from two packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), both Creative Commons Zero (CC0), public domain:
free to use in any project, with no credit required. Credited here with thanks.

  Interface Sounds (1.0), 2020: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
  Sci-Fi Sounds (1.0), 2020: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
  Digital Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio

Her footsteps (step-1..4) are from Kenney's RPG Audio (above). The rain — its bed (bed-rain) and its drops (drop-1..3) —
is from "Rain (loopable)" by Ylmir on OpenGameArt, also CC0:
  https://opengameart.org/content/rain-loopable
  License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The other ambient beds (bed-city, bed-room) have no pack: they are made from shaped noise by tools/sounds/import-world.py,
so they loop with no seam. The HUD's window cues (window-open, window-close) are made from shaped noise by
tools/sounds/synth-hud.py.

sources.json says which file each sound here was made from; the tools in tools/sounds/ made them (trimmed, levelled,
looped, written as WAV).
