The interface's sounds are from two packs by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), both Creative Commons Zero (CC0), public domain:
free to use in any project, with no credit required. Credited here with thanks.

  Interface Sounds (1.0), 2020: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  RPG Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
  Sci-Fi Sounds (1.0), 2020: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
  Digital Audio: https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio

The ambient beds (bed-rain, bed-city, bed-room) have no pack: they are made from shaped noise by
tools/sounds/import-world.py, so they loop with no seam.
  License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

sources.json says which file each sound here was made from; tools/sounds/import-kenney.py made them (trimmed, levelled,
written as WAV).
