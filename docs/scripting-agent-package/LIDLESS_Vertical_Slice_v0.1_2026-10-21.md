# LIDLESS — Vertical slice v0.1
**21 October 2026 · a proposal for the director: the 30 to 45 minute slice, built from what plays now**

Nothing here changes the bible. Each story choice is marked **Decide**. Where something must be made, the time is my
own working time.

## What plays now (about 10 to 15 minutes)

- **Her flat**, a privacy sanctuary:
  - the parcel on her desk, due at the radio repair by ten;
  - her radio, one valve dead;
  - the photo: raw view shows someone beside her whom the Concierge removed;
  - the wardrobe, the shower and the notebook.
- **The main street:**
  - the radio repair and the fixer;
  - the nutrition station, where people come and go;
  - the guardian on its round;
  - the bar with the restaurant above it, the cocktail bar and the corner shop;
  - the public restroom, a privacy room the Concierge notices if she uses it too long or too often;
  - drones and the train.
- **The backstreet**, and the radio repair inside.
- **The loop that plays:**
  1. She delivers the parcel.
  2. The fixer switches on her raw view, and she owes him a favour.
  3. In raw view, the "curio" on her desk is a radio valve.
  4. Her radio works, and under the static there is a voice that is not the Concierge's.
- **Systems built:**
  - conversations with choices, and story commands (walk, wear, go, close-ups);
  - things to take and use on each other, as props;
  - the two views, with the Concierge's attention;
  - privacy rooms, the analog notebook and the device journal;
  - the map, with places found and quick travel;
  - **the old terminal** (Relay 7, a placeholder);
  - the title screen, settings and saves.

## The slice, in three beats

### 1. The errand (built, about 10 minutes)

As it plays now. It ends with the voice under the static.

### 2. The voice at three (to build, about 15 minutes)

- **The voice comes clearest at night.** She waits in her flat until three; the bed is the natural place for it.
- **At three, the radio gives a pattern:** a voice reading numbers, then a tone. She writes them in her notebook, and
  she can only open the notebook in a privacy zone (her flat is one).
- **She asks the fixer.** He knows the band: "Municipal. There was a relay in the Eastern District, before." And the
  favour she owes comes due.
- **The old computer reaches her.** The director's direction (22 October, not decided in the story yet): she comes by
  it easily, as **a retro laptop**, and it may be where she learns about the raw-view mod, or something like it. Ways
  it could reach her, still to choose:
  - **(a)** The fixer has kept it in his back room for years and cannot get past its password. Its price is the
    favour: "Take it home. Find out what it knows."
  - **(b)** She finds it in the backstreet, in a skip of electronics waiting to be collected.
  - **(c)** It comes from someone connected to the person in the photo.
- **She carries it home.** A laptop is easily carried. It becomes a prop on her desk, and she opens it there. Its
  screen could then look like an old laptop's (a monochrome LCD) rather than the green-glass monitor it has now.

### 3. The machine and the map (to build, about 15 minutes)

- **The terminal**, with Relay 7's placeholder content as it stands:
  - the log shows an unscheduled carrier at 03:00 on 7350 kHz, the band she heard;
  - a careless shell history gives away root's password;
  - the handover note says the relay's entry in the district registry "will be closed";
  - the night operator's notes give the same numbers she wrote down.
- **The deduction.** The relay's address, from the machine, does not match the Concierge's map: in its view there is
  a blank wall, "utility, no access". In raw view there is a door. Worked out, the place appears on her map, sketched
  in.
- **The slice ends at that door.** That is a meaningful but incomplete discovery, as the bible asks. **Decide**
  whether she goes through it in the slice or in chapter 1.
- **An optional social lead.** The night operator, Mara Lind, would be about 90 now. The public restroom is where
  they could talk unheard. **Decide** whether she, or someone else, belongs in the slice.

## Decisions for the director

1. **Luna's motive.** The photo says she is looking for someone the Concierge removed. Is that her motive in the slice,
   and who are they to her?
2. **The old computer.** A retro laptop she comes by easily, perhaps where she learns about the raw-view mod
   (direction, 22 October). How does it reach her: (a), (b), (c), or another way? And does the raw view then come
   from what she finds on it rather than from the fixer?
3. **The first mystery.** Relay 7, the three o'clock pattern and the closed registry entry follow the bible's proposed
   central mystery (a municipal signal or address that is not on today's maps). Keep that shape, or should the
   machine hold something else?
4. **The year.** The machine's files are from 2030, just before Care, by the bible's timeline. Is that right?
5. **The ending.** Does the slice end at the door, or go through it?
6. **The meeting.** Is there a meeting in the restroom in the slice, and with whom?
